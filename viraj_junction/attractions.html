Our Attractions

Over 20 thrilling rides and zones for every kind of visitor.

🌊

Wave Pool

Experience the thrill of ocean waves in our massive wave pool — great for all age groups.

🌀

Tornado Slide

A high-speed spiral slide that sends you into a splash pool at the bottom.

🚷

Lazy River

Float along our 300-metre lazy river on an inflatable ring — pure relaxation.

⛷

Family Raft Ride

A group ride on a giant raft through twists and turns — perfect for families.

📧

Kids' Splash Zone

A dedicated safe zone for little ones with mini slides, water sprays, and splash pads.

⚡

Kamikaze Slide

Our steepest drop slide — not for the faint-hearted! 15 metres of pure adrenaline.

🌧

Rain Dance

Open-air dance floor with overhead rain showers and music — a crowd favourite.

📦

Speed Slides

Three parallel speed slides — race your friends to the bottom!
